# Executive Presentation

## Slide 01 — Couture Atelier Intelligence

**Decision support for desirability, availability, and client relevance.**

One-line context: public retail observations, transformed into a clearly labeled synthetic luxury operating model.

## Slide 02 — The executive question

How should a luxury house decide:

- What demand is emerging?
- Where should scarce inventory move?
- Which clients merit attention?
- When is no action the most respectful action?

## Slide 03 — The system

Observed public data feeds analytical marts. A synthetic luxury layer adds boutiques, product attributes, consent, and inventory. Decision layers connect forecasting, allocation, CLV, churn, segments, and clienteling.

## Slide 04 — Demand signal

The feature-based model improves MAE from 6.88 to 5.79 on the same chronological holdout. Emphasize that this is a methodological benchmark on public retail data, not a claim about private luxury-house performance.

## Slide 05 — Availability

The constrained optimizer recommends 13 transfers totaling 59 units plus 200 warehouse replenishment units, reducing projected stockouts by 0.49 percentage points. Clearly state that transfer cost and lead time remain scenario refinements.

## Slide 06 — Client value

The CLV baseline reaches 0.686 correlation with realized next-90-day revenue. Churn prediction achieves 0.816 ROC-AUC on a later time-based cohort.

## Slide 07 — Clienteling with restraint

The system creates 5,942 decisions, including 1,176 explicit no-action outcomes. Consent is a hard gate. Recommendations are associate decision support, not automatic outreach.
